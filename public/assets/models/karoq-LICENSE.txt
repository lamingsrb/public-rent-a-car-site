Public Rent a Car presentation modifications:
Embedded RGB maps recompressed to JPEG quality 92, 4:4:4, at original dimensions
(maximum 1024 pixels). Maps with alpha are preserved. Geometry, UVs and meshopt
geometry buffers are unchanged from the archive GLB. Runtime scale, orientation,
silver paint response, lighting, contact shadow, camera, road and abstract
architectural frames are adapted. SHA-256 hashes and image changes are recorded
in the adjacent karoq-manifest.json.
The source registration graphics are hidden at runtime and replaced on both
ends by a code-drawn Serbian BG 099-ST graphic using one shared CanvasTexture.
This is an illustrative registration, not the actual rental vehicle's plate.
The GLB geometry and UVs are unchanged. Runtime presentation also adds a
36-metre route journey and rotation of the four original centred wheel groups.

Variant distinction:
This asset depicts a 2019 Karoq in the same pre-facelift body family as the
client's 2021 Karoq Sportline 1.5 TSI. It is not a scan of their individual car.
Trim, wheels, paint and interior details may differ. Actual rental vehicle
photographs and confirmed specifications remain in the fleet catalogue.
