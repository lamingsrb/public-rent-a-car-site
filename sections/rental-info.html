How the rental works
  1. Choose a car from the fleet.
  2. Send your dates and pick-up point, or call us to ask about the price and availability.
  3. Bring a passport or ID card and your driving licence at pick-up. You sign a rental contract; copies of your documents are kept for the rental records.
  4. Collect and return the car at an agreed location. Each rental day covers 24 hours, with a 1-hour return tolerance.

Deposit, payment and rental conditions

The driver must be at least 21 and have held a valid licence for at least 2 years. A deposit is mandatory at pick-up: minimum €300, depending on the vehicle.

Prices are in euros; payment is made in dinars at the Banka Intesa selling rate at pick-up. All payment cards are accepted.

Regular clients can receive a reduced deposit. The deposit can be paid in any currency, in proportion to the euro exchange rate.

If your chosen car is unavailable, a car of the same or a higher class may be provided under the same conditions.

Insurance terms are not summarised on this page. Message us and ask about them.
