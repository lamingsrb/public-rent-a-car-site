07 · Terms

The terms, in numbers.

  • 21+

    Driver age

    The driver must be at least 21 years old.

  • 2 yrs

    Licence held

    The driver must have held a valid licence for at least 2 years.

  • €300+

    Deposit

    A deposit is paid at pick-up. It depends on the vehicle, with a minimum of €300.

  • 24 h

    Rental day

    The daily price covers 24 hours from pick-up, with up to 1 hour of tolerance on return.

  • €25

    Leaving Serbia

    Possible with our consent and for a €25 fee.

  • RSD

    Payment

    Prices are in euros; payment is made in dinars at the Banka Intesa selling rate at pick-up. All payment cards are accepted.

Fine print

At pick-up

Bring a passport or ID card and your driving licence. We copy and keep them for our records, and you sign a rental contract.

If your car is unavailable

If the selected car is unavailable, we may provide a car of the same or a higher class under the same conditions.

Deposit details

Regular clients can receive a reduced deposit. The deposit can be paid in any currency, in proportion to the euro exchange rate.

Leaving Serbia

Taking the vehicle out of Serbia needs our consent and a €25 fee.

Insurance

Insurance terms are not summarised on this page. Message us and ask about them.
